## Why brand protection belongs in the entry plan

Trademark filings are cheap relative to everything else in a market entry, and they are time-sensitive in a way most founders discover too late: most of Asia operates on a first-to-file basis, where the person who registers first generally wins, whatever the history of use elsewhere. The right moment to protect the brand is before the market knows you are coming, not after.

Meridium's role is to make protection systematic: clearance before commitment, a specification that matches the business plan, filings sequenced across your markets, and a register that keeps renewals from slipping. Where a matter needs a registered IP agent or a disputes lawyer, we say so plainly and manage the specialist, so you get the right expertise without running another vendor relationship.
