## From first hire to steady state

Employment in Singapore is well regulated and unforgiving of improvisation: key employment terms must be issued in writing, salaries must be paid on time with itemised payslips, CPF contributions are due monthly for local employees, and employment income must be reported annually on the IR8A. None of this is difficult; all of it is mandatory. We set it up once, correctly, and then run it as a quiet monthly cycle.

Where your corporate bank account is still in progress, salary disbursement runs through Meridium's local bank account under a documented arrangement, so hiring is never hostage to bank onboarding.

## Work passes: getting your people here

Every foreign hire needs the right pass, and the right pass depends on the person. The figures below are the current published criteria from the Ministry of Manpower (MOM), stated as at the time of writing; MOM revises them periodically, and increases already announced take effect for new applications from 1 January 2027. We confirm the applicable figures before every application.

### Employment Pass

The standard pass for professionals, managers and executives. Eligibility runs in two stages: a minimum qualifying salary, currently from S$5,600 a month for the youngest candidates rising progressively with age to S$10,700 at age 45 and above (higher in financial services, from S$6,200 to S$11,800), and then the COMPASS points framework, where an application must score 40 points on attributes of the candidate and the employing firm. MOM's published processing time is within ten business days for most online applications from Singapore-registered employers.
<!-- Verified 12 Aug 2026: https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility and .../apply-for-a-pass -->

### S Pass

For mid-skilled staff. The qualifying salary currently runs from S$3,300 a month rising with age to S$4,800 at age 45 and above (financial services from S$3,800 to S$5,650). Unlike the Employment Pass, the S Pass carries a workforce quota, currently 10 per cent of the company's total workforce in services and 15 per cent in construction, manufacturing, marine shipyard and process sectors, and a monthly foreign worker levy of S$650. MOM's published processing time is within ten business days for most online applications.
<!-- Verified 12 Aug 2026: https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass/eligibility and .../quota-and-levy/levy-and-quota-requirements -->

### Personalised Employment Pass

A pass held by the individual rather than tied to one employer, suited to senior professionals who want flexibility between roles. It currently requires a fixed monthly salary of at least S$22,500 at application, and holders must go on earning at least S$270,000 in fixed salary each calendar year. The pass runs up to three years and is issued once, without renewal. MOM's published processing time is within six weeks for most cases.
<!-- Verified 12 Aug 2026: https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/personalised-employment-pass/eligibility and .../key-facts -->

### Overseas Networks and Expertise (ONE) Pass

Singapore's pass for top-tier talent. The salary route requires a fixed monthly salary of at least S$30,000 over the twelve months before applying (candidates employed overseas must also come from an employer of substantial scale), and an alternative route exists for individuals with outstanding achievements in sports, arts and culture, or academia and research. The pass runs five years and is renewable. MOM's published processing time is within four weeks for most cases.
<!-- Verified 12 Aug 2026: https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/eligibility and .../key-facts -->

### EntrePass

For foreign founders building venture-backed or innovative technology businesses in Singapore. There is no minimum salary; instead the founder must hold at least 30 per cent of a Singapore private limited company and show substance through one of several routes: at least S$100,000 raised from recognised investors, support from a government-recognised or internationally renowned incubator or accelerator, registered intellectual property, research collaboration with a local institution, or a track record of having founded and sold a venture-backed business. MOM's published processing time is within six weeks for most cases.
<!-- Verified 12 Aug 2026: https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/entrepass/eligibility and .../apply-for-a-pass -->

## Renewals, dependants and departures

A pass is not a one-off event. Renewals fall due, family members need Dependant's Passes or Long-Term Visit Passes, and employees eventually leave, which triggers cancellation and, for foreign staff, tax clearance before the final salary is released. All of it sits on the same compliance calendar as the rest of your file, watched by the same named contact.
