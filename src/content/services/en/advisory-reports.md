## Written advice as a discipline

A recommendation that must survive in writing is held to a higher standard than one delivered across a table. Committing the analysis to a document forces the options to be genuinely compared, the assumptions to be stated, and the recommendation to be owned. That discipline is the product as much as the conclusion is.

The reports are senior work throughout: scoped by the person who writes them, written by the person who defends them. Where a question reaches into a specific market, we draw on the partner network that serves our [Asia expansion](/services/asia-expansion/) work, so the view from Jakarta or Mumbai in the report comes from someone who practises there, not from a search engine.
